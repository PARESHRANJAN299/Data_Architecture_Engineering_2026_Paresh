# Stakeholder Summary

## What changes in Phase 1?

The company moves from static files and manual examples to a continuously operating event platform.

## What becomes possible immediately?

- live product ingestion
- live customer and order simulation
- near-real-time operational visibility
- replayable raw history
- a foundation for sales, product, inventory, BI, and ML use cases

## What does Phase 1 not deliver yet?

Phase 1 does not provide final business KPIs or predictive models.

It creates the reliable data foundation required to build them correctly.

## Why this sequence is important

Building dashboards before trusted ingestion creates fast but unreliable answers.

Building the raw event path first ensures that every later KPI and model can be traced back to original events.
